Leaking Pipe Water Damage in Spokane: What to Know

You seldom catch a pipe leak the day it begins. Water seeps out behind drywall, at the back of a sink cabinet or under the concrete until a stain, a soft floor or an unexplained water bill gives it away. At that point the drywall and wood nearby may have been soaking for weeks. We serve Spokane homeowners by tracing the full wet area and drying every bit of it, including what is out of sight.

Fixing the pipe ends the leak, yet the moisture already inside the wall or floor stays put. Getting it out is the work we do. Our crew maps the damp materials with thermal imaging and moisture meters, opens up no more than necessary, and runs dehumidifiers and air movers until the readings return to normal. Mold often gets a head start during a long leak, which is why we look for it and treat the surfaces involved.

Your First Steps

  1. Shut Off the WaterWhen a toilet or sink line is dripping, shut the valve on the supply right at that fixture. Not sure where it is coming from? Close the main shutoff for the whole house.
  2. Keep Power AwayWater around a switch, outlet or light fixture means the breaker for that part of the house should go off. Keep your hands off cords and devices that have gotten wet.
  3. Check the Water MeterMake sure nothing in the house is using water and read the meter. Movement on the dial means water is escaping out of sight, perhaps beneath the slab.
  4. Empty and PhotographBefore a repair is made or anything goes in the trash, remove what is stored near the leak and get pictures of the pipe, the staining and the warping.
  5. Have the Area MeteredBefore the opening is sealed, get the surrounding materials checked with a moisture meter. Drywall can feel dry to the touch while the cavity behind it is still damp.
Insurance

Insurance and Leaking Pipe Water Damage

Insurance treats slow leaks differently from sudden ones. Policies often cover damage that is sudden and accidental. A leak lasting weeks or months usually falls outside that, and certain policies name it as an exclusion. If the leak was concealed and you could not have known, it may be viewed more favorably than a visible leak nobody dealt with. Read your policy and talk to your agent. We document the source of the leak, moisture readings and photos so the adjuster has clear facts.

What It Costs

What Drives the Price

  • Access to the pipe, with a sink cabinet being cheaper to work in than a slab or a closed wall.
  • Which finishes and flooring got wet, as some can be saved by drying and some cannot.
  • The amount of flooring, cabinetry, insulation or drywall that must come out and be replaced.
  • How long the pipe leaked and how far the moisture spread from it.

The Usual Culprits

Why This Happens

  • High water pressure that stresses joints and valves year after year until one weeps.
  • Leaks at a sink drain joint or trap, which release a little water with every use.
  • A hairline split left by freezing weather in a line running through a crawl space or outside wall.
  • Water lines under a concrete slab that get rubbed through or stressed as the soil moves.
  • Loose or worn compression fittings and supply connections under sinks and behind toilets.
Why Speed Matters

What the Water Damages

Hidden Mold Growth

Mold thrives in a wall cavity that is both damp and dark. With a slow leak it often covers the back of the drywall before a single spot shows on the painted side.

Rotted Framing

Wall studs and bottom plates that stay damp for weeks begin to soften and decay. Once framing lumber is no longer sound, it must be removed and new wood put in.

Damaged Flooring

Moisture from a leaking slab or wall line spreads out below the finished floor. Hardwood cups, laminate swells at the seams, and adhesive under vinyl and tile lets go.

Ceiling and Drywall Damage

Leaks over a ceiling or behind a wall surface show as bubbling paint and brown stains. Drywall that stays wet goes soft, sags and may need to be cut out.

How We Handle Leaking Pipe Water Damage

Find Every Wet Area

Using a thermal camera and moisture meters, our crew checks cabinets, floors and walls and marks each wet spot, even those far from the pipe that water reached by running along the framing.

2

Small, planned openings give us access to wet cavities. Insulation, drywall or cabinet pieces beyond saving come out, and moldy material is bagged to keep spores from spreading through the house.

Dry the Structure

Drying equipment stays in place, typically for 3 to 5 days, until moisture in the framing and subfloor is back to normal. Readings are taken at each visit, and the setup is changed as the materials dry.

Final Treatment and Records

We apply antimicrobial treatment to affected surfaces and give you moisture logs and photos. Those records show the area was dry before repairs and help with any claim.
