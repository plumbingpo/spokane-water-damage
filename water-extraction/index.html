Water extraction is the most critical first step in any water damage restoration project. The faster standing water is removed from your property, the less damage occurs to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems. With each hour it sits, water works farther in: through carpet to the pad, up the drywall, into wood framing and down to the subfloor. Across Spokane, Spokane Restoration Co handles emergency extraction with industrial portable and truck-mounted machines that can remove hundreds of gallons a minute.

Our truck-mounted extraction units are among the most powerful water removal systems available, with the suction to pull water from carpets, hard floors and structural materials far more effectively than consumer or rental equipment. Where the truck hoses will not reach, we bring in submersible pumps, strong portable extractors and specialty tools made for water caught between floor layers, inside wall cavities or in tight spaces.

Professional water extraction is not simply about removing the standing water you can see. Our IICRC-certified technicians use moisture detection equipment to identify water that has migrated into hidden areas: behind baseboards, inside wall cavities, under cabinets and beneath flooring materials. Specialty extraction tools remove as much moisture as possible from these hidden areas before the drying phase begins, which significantly reduces the time and equipment needed for structural drying and lowers the risk of secondary mold growth.

Why Hire a Professional for Water Extraction?

Both the harm done to your home and the restoration bill depend directly on quick, thorough extraction. Truck-mounted extractors move water at hundreds of times the rate of consumer tools, and a job that could cost you a full day with a shop vac is done by our machines in minutes. This speed difference is not just about convenience, it is about preventing thousands of dollars in additional damage that occurs with every hour water stays in contact with building materials.

Hidden moisture, the kind consumer tools never reach, is part of a professional extraction too. Through capillary action and wicking, water moves up drywall, into the pad under carpet, between layers of flooring and along plumbing lines and electrical conduit. We go after those hidden deposits with specialty tools and methods, and that takes out far more water than extracting the surface only. This thorough approach reduces drying time, lowers equipment costs and, most importantly, prevents the hidden mold growth that results from trapped moisture.

Water Extraction Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Check the supply hoses on your appliances and swap out any that look old or worn
  • Find the main shutoff valve for the house and put a clear label on it
  • Early leak signs show up near appliances, toilets and sink cabinets, so check those spots on a schedule
  • Raise anything you store in the basement a few inches above the floor
  • Program (423) 546-7409 into your phone so you can call immediately in an emergency
  • Keep a basic emergency water kit: old towels, a bucket and your restoration company's number

Warning Signs for Water Extraction

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Carpeting so wet it squelches when you step on it or has puddles on top
  • Water squeezing up between floorboards, planks or tiles when you walk
  • Water entering through a flooded basement stairwell or window well
  • Water seeping out from under baseboards or cabinets
  • Sprinkler discharge that has left water on floors and contents
  • An appliance, HVAC unit or pipe that is leaking right now
  • A utility area, mechanical room or crawl space with water in it
