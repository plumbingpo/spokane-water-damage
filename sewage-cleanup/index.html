Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Raw sewage contains dangerous bacteria, viruses, parasites and other pathogens, including E. coli, hepatitis, salmonella and giardia, that pose serious health risks to anyone who touches contaminated materials. Across Spokane, Spokane Restoration Co answers sewage emergencies 24/7, sending trained technicians who arrive with EPA-registered disinfectants and the right protective equipment so your property is decontaminated safely.

The industry classifies sewage as black water, or Category 3, which is the most dangerous and most contaminated type of water damage there is. This classification requires the most stringent cleanup protocols, including full personal protective equipment, containment barriers, removal of all porous materials that contacted sewage and thorough disinfection of every remaining surface. Our technicians follow IICRC S500 standards for Category 3 water damage so your property is restored to safe occupancy standards.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. To finish, thermal foggers and hydroxyl generators of industrial grade deodorize the space so the sewage smell does not linger.

Typical culprits are tree roots entering the pipe, grease hardening inside it and wipes or similar items flushed down a toilet. Sewage Cleanup Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Clear tree roots near sewer lines, which can infiltrate and block the pipes
  • Every 2 to 3 years, get a sewer camera run through the line to spot developing issues
  • Never pour grease, oil or food waste down your drains, since these cause blockages
  • Install a backwater valve on your sewer connection to prevent backups from the municipal system
  • In an emergency a plumber needs the cleanout, so learn where yours is and keep it in good shape
  • Use drain strainers in showers and sinks to catch hair and debris

Why Sewage Cleanup Is Not a DIY Job

Sewage cleanup is not something any homeowner should attempt. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. Exposure to sewage pathogens can cause serious illness, including gastrointestinal disease, hepatitis, respiratory infections and skin infections.

Disposal matters as well, since everything the sewage contaminated has to be handled as biohazard waste. Simply placing sewage-contaminated drywall, carpet and insulation in your regular trash is both illegal and hazardous to sanitation workers and the community. A certified restoration company follows proper disposal protocols and works with licensed facilities that accept biohazard waste.

Signs You Need Sewage Cleanup in Spokane

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • A toilet overflow containing waste that has spread onto flooring or carpet
  • Soggy, smelly ground over the sewer line or leakage at the cleanout cap
  • Dark residue from sewage left on the basement floor or ringing a drain
  • Lower levels or drain openings giving off a heavy sewage odor, sometimes like rotten eggs
  • Damp patches on a wall or floor that also smell foul
  • Toilets or drains that gurgle whenever you run water somewhere else
  • Family members falling ill, especially with gastrointestinal trouble, for no obvious reason
